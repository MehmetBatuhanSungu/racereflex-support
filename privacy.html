Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Race Reflex app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app has no sign-up, no email and no password, and it collects only what it needs to run the daily leaderboard, send optional reminders and show ads.

Your anonymous player account

When you first open the app it creates an anonymous player account for you automatically. It is identified only by a random ID, which is stored in your device's Keychain so it survives app updates. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

Data we collect

  • Race results. The reaction time of every completed race and when it happened, linked to your anonymous ID. We use these for the daily leaderboard, your personal best, and to understand how the game is played (for example, how many races a typical session has). Jump starts are not sent.
  • Display name. An automatically generated name (such as "Racer1a2b"), or the name you choose on the leaderboard.
  • Push notification token and language (optional). Only if you allow notifications: your device's push token and the app language (English or Turkish), so we can send reminders such as "the daily leaderboard resets soon" in your language.

What other players can see

The daily leaderboard is visible to everyone who uses the app. It shows your display name, today's best time and your all-time best. Don't choose a display name that identifies you if you'd rather stay anonymous.

Advertising

The app shows full-screen ads between races using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, which ads were shown and tapped, and diagnostic data (for example, ad crashes and performance). The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not give Google access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). In regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the app's backend (anonymous accounts, race results, leaderboard, push tokens).
  • Apple delivers push notifications (Apple Push Notification service).
  • Google provides ads (AdMob).

We do not sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone other than these providers, who process it to run the services above.

Data stored on your device

Besides your anonymous account key, the app keeps a few settings on your device, such as whether sound is on and the latest ad settings it downloaded. These stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Your choices

  • Notifications: you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Race Reflex → Notifications.
  • Display name: you can change it at any time from the leaderboard.
  • Deletion: to have your race results and display name deleted, email us with your display name. If you delete the app, your anonymous account can no longer be accessed from your device.

Children's privacy

Race Reflex is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change.
