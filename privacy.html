Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Race Reflex app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Your privacy matters, and the app is built to need as little of your data as possible.

Data we collect

None. Race Reflex does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no backend server.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best reaction time locally on your device so it can be shown to you between sessions. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party services, trackers, or SDKs. No data is sent to Apple or anyone else by the app itself. Standard, aggregate metrics that Apple may provide to developers (such as anonymised download or crash counts) are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy and cannot identify you.

Children's privacy

Race Reflex does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if analytics or ads are added in a future version), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change.
