Contact

How to play

  1. Tap to start the sequence.
  2. Watch the five red lights fill up one by one.
  3. Wait through the random hold — do not anticipate.
  4. Tap the instant all lights go out.
  5. Try to beat your best time.

Tapping before the lights go out counts as a jump start and no time is recorded for that round.

FAQ

My best time disappeared.

The best time is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app, or moving to a new device, resets it.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Race Reflex works fully offline and needs no account.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

What is a good reaction time?

Typical human reaction time is around 200–250 milliseconds. Under about 150 ms usually means you anticipated the lights rather than reacted.
